## Source confirmation

Endgame guidance was checked visually against the Facilitator Guide, PDF page 21 / printed page 19. The component gives the Ring three repair requirements: ten materials, one Engineering Shuttle and five different eligible research tracks at four crosses. Each vessel pays five fuel for passage. A far-side sabotage decision prevents pursuit through the Ring. Nebula attempts use one server die, accumulated scouting at that location and earlier vessel losses there; nine is the success threshold. An unsuccessful ship is lost and helps later attempts. The Station requires eliminating the entire occupying Wolf force, including immediate repeated attacks against survivors. Activation requires at least eighteen available Reactor charge capacity from cannibalised vessels. Initial Station pressure is at least one Battlestation plus twenty damage from other Wolf vessels.

## Digital choices left open by the printed component

- Contributions use current server inventories, research and craft ownership. Research qualification does not erase lab crosses; it records five distinct eligible tracks for this repair. An Engineering Shuttle is reserved once for Ring repair and cannot fund another site. Materials are deducted atomically. Repair completes when the last requirement commits.
- Ring passage is chosen and paid separately by each vessel. The GM makes an explicit far-side sabotage call after at least one paid crossing. Its operational view lists vessels that have not crossed. Sabotage records their left-behind disposition without inventing a population death or arrival. This is a deliberate cutoff policy, not an automatic consequence of the first crossing.
- Nebula scouting and earlier losses are counted at the physical candidate coordinate, including prior losses from another group there. They stay server-private. Each vessel has one durable reached-or-lost result. A lost vessel becomes destroyed, its current survivor ledger becomes zero and its old ship actions close. Replays preserve the original die and cost.
- Separate groups retain their own candidate choices. A group succeeds at the Nebula only after each current group vessel has a reached/lost disposition or was already destroyed, and at least one reached safety. The global session closes only when every active fleet vessel has a disposition or was already destroyed. An all-lost fleet uses the existing total-fleet-loss outcome. Mixed settled groups do not cause a first-vessel fleet-wide success.
- Station liberation is a durable private marker written only by the source-bound Wolf finalizer after its verified receipt has no surviving Wolves and its repeat plan stops. A later unrelated attack cannot erase it. Reactors count their current undamaged/upgraded available capacity once. A cannibalised ship closes its vessel actions while its living survivor ledger remains intact. Activation settles the current group at the Station and changes its operational title to New Eden Station.
- The shared ending helper receives the actual post-consequence survivor aggregate. That aggregate is current population accounting; it does not claim that a living left-behind vessel reached New Eden. Candidate group summaries retain the separate disposition.

## Integration contracts

`readCandidateWorkspace` is an ordinary authenticated callable. A player supplies the current group; a GM additionally supplies an owned live instance. The server returns only discoveries known to that group, costs, requirements, blockers, next actor and group vessel results. Only the GM receives private calculation records. Hidden scout totals, arithmetic and foreign vessel lists are excluded from player replies.

`executeCandidateAction` accepts one exact chosen action, current group, candidate identity, expected candidate revision and request ID, plus only that action's human choices. It accepts no dice or calculated outcome. The server checks a bound receipt before new-action phase/settlement guards. Exact retries, including after debrief, return the retained result. A changed actor, payload or group cannot reuse the receipt.

`serverState/candidates`, `serverState/candidateStationLiberations`, `candidateActionReceipts` and `candidateCalculationAudits` are server-owned. New mutations also create the shared private `actionAudits`/`procedureAudits` records once. Readable player documents contain only the minimal `candidateDisposition: {code, outcome, cycle}`. Detailed vessel disposition stays in GM-only root/server authority.

`commitTerminal` owns the immutable debrief and lifecycle transition in the candidate transaction. The candidate caller supplies a minimal public success marker, group evidence with audience labels, post-consequence survivor accounting and the allowed `candidateVesselOutcomes` patch. It does not write debrief state independently. Public success markers have only type, result, cause, candidate code, cycle and timestamp.

The existing shared player ship-action guard rejects a settled candidate disposition. Parent integration must adopt root `candidateVesselOutcomes` checks for standalone services and GM vessel actions that do not use this guard, preserving exact receipt recovery before checking new action availability. Optional admitted small-vessel authority uses its existing maintenance and host ledger; this group does not manufacture a new core crew seat or source profile.

## Executable fresh route

The fresh authenticated candidate driver is `scripts/test-pc10-candidates-authenticated.mjs`. It creates and casts a normal session, runs real charged maintenance, reads the entitled GM chart, chooses actual jumps, requests and resolves real scouts, uses the connected player button, settles every vessel and recovers original receipts after debrief. Its bounded local proof supplies resource counters through ordinary GM actions and accelerates only Team clock deadlines; it never seeds navigation, scout markers, candidate state, rolls or outcomes. In the integrated full-game demo, use its ordinary manual Team/Coordination controls in place of the clock acceleration.

For a fresh Ring path, the Scientist advances five selected tracks once per Team Phase across four cycles: three standard choices and two five-ore choices per cycle, forty ore total. Crews then arrive, commit the required materials/craft/research, choose each paid passage and ask the GM for the explicit far-side cutoff. For a fresh Station path, arrival produces its P source pressure; stage the valid source force, declare the attack, resolve actual role-owned combat choices and repeat the finalized survivor roster until empty before any Reactor contribution. Prepared snapshots or direct terminal writes do not prove either path.
