## After version 1.0

### Review the Wolf hacking alert experience

- **Source:** Prompt 503a and the product-owner decision of 2026-09-23.
- **Campaign behavior to ship:** A committed console or supply sabotage raises
  an alert on the GM view wherever the GM is in the app. After the GM
  acknowledges the alert and its clue decision, the existing approved hacking
  overlay is sent automatically to every player console. The server owns the
  trigger and acknowledgment; no private Wolf identity, action detail,
  suspicion value, or clue result enters the player notice. The overlay remains
  nonblocking, hidden from assistive technology, and respectful of reduced
  motion.
- **Post-1.0 task:** Review this table-wide presentation after real play. Check
  whether its audience, frequency, and GM acknowledgment timing help the game
  without revealing more than the adjudicated clue. Decide whether to retain,
  narrow, revise, or remove the overlay, then record the decision and any
  follow-up implementation and release evidence here.
- **Status:** Scheduled for review after 1.0; Prompt 503a implementation and
  production-path proof remain campaign work.
